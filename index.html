Voice for Maharashtra's medicos

Your voice shouldn't get lost in the system.

Long hours, heavy syllabus, stipend drama, hostel issues. You deal with it all, and mostly alone. MMSA exists so medical students across the state can speak together, learn together and actually get heard.
